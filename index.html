01 / ABOUT

About

I am a mathematics researcher at the University of Padova, working at the intersection of analysis, geometric variational problems and optimal transport. My interests include nonlocal energies, fractional geometric problems and Wasserstein gradient flows.

This website collects my research publications and other selected projects.
